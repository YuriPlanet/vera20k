# Proposal for preset loading, terrain and ground movement

**English** · [简体中文](preset-terrain.zh-CN.md) · [Deutsch](preset-terrain.de.md)

**Status** — Proposed for upstream discussion. The first change offered to VERA20K is to load a theater definition independently of its MIX archives. VERA20K can supply that definition from the retail INI it selects today. The downstream VERA Mod Engine can supply it from a versioned preset. Both use the same tile resolver, map loader and simulation. The downstream product decisions below apply to that fork. [VERA Mod Engine](https://github.com/YuriPlanet/vera-mod-engine)

## First change for upstream

`load_theater` currently mounts the theater archives, selects a control INI, parses the numbered tilesets and `[General]` roles, resolves tile variants and loads the palettes. Desktop map loading, headless play and cold save loading all call it. Multiplayer also consults the selected theater INIs and changed tiles when deciding whether two installations can play together. [Theater loader](../../src/map/theater.rs) · [Headless scenario](../../src/headless_scenario.rs) · [Player save loading](../../src/app/persistence/resource_load.rs) · [Network agreement](../Networking.md)

1. Extract the ordered tilesets and theater roles into a typed definition. Keep the original tileset ordinals, tile ranges, signed bridge-piece values and the distinction between an absent role and tile zero. Provide an adapter for upstream's existing INI selection, including a mod's theater override. Give the loader a definition and a read-only asset source.
2. Resolve TMP tiles and their variants, palettes and any other named assets against that source. A tile's land, height and traversability can affect play. Bind such metadata before calculating the match rules identity. Report a missing theater definition separately from a missing tile or palette, using the existing fallback where the retail profile specifies one.
3. Exercise both sources on `TEMPERATE`. Load the same map with the same assets through the desktop and headless entrances, then cold-load a save. Compare actual tile IDs and subtiles, ground heights, bridge pieces and movement results. In the preset case, deny requests for the loose theater INI and its MIX member. Measuring that first cut separately matters because rules, AI and audio still have their own readers. [Map fixture](../../tests/maps/match.map) · [Theater reader tests](../../src/map/theater_tests.rs)

## Terrain seen by the rest of the game

A cell beneath a high bridge can have a water surface, an intact deck above it and objects on both layers. A tank's route, a boat's route, a building foundation and a shell's contact test ask different questions about that cell. Each needs the same committed geometry and its own admission rules.

**Proposed query.** Resolve a map cell and the requested surface (ground or bridge deck) to its current geometry, elevation and land class. An absent deck is a distinct result. Movement adds its traversal costs and layer transitions, placement examines its foundation, and projectile contact uses its segment and height. For an ordinary FA2 map, resolve the existing tile and subtile ordinals through the selected theater. Derived indexes refer back to the same cells. A preset can define a surface that a rover may cross and on which a building cannot be placed. Those choices do not require a new FA2 map section. [Map format](../map-format.md) · [Projectile contact](../../src/sim/world/projectile_collision.rs)

| Question | Source and responsibility |
|---|---|
| Which surface exists at this cell and height? | `ResolvedTerrainGrid` supplies live land, slope, ground height and deck facts. The theater supplies tile meanings. `BridgeRuntimeState` and its existing damage and repair operations own the structural change. [Cell surfaces](../../src/map/resolved_terrain.rs) · [Bridge state](../../src/sim/bridge_state/mod.rs) |
| Can this unit reach and enter the requested layer? | Navigation uses the movement definition, layer and current connectivity. Admission also checks the relevant ground or deck occupants. Movement commits position and occupancy in its existing object turn. The object substrate owns cell membership and order. [Movement](../../src/sim/world/object_turn.rs) · [Navigation](../../src/sim/world/navigation.rs) · [Occupancy](../../src/sim/world/substrate.rs) |
| Can a building go here? What does a hit affect? | Placement checks every cell of the foundation and its placement rules. Preview and the build command use the same ground facts, and the command rechecks current occupancy. Combat selects the eligible surface, object or bridge at impact. Walls and ore are overlays, trees have object identity and health, and smudges have their own lifetime. Their respective owners apply the result. [Placement](../../src/sim/production/production_placement.rs) · [Overlays](../../src/sim/overlay_grid.rs) · [Terrain objects](../../src/sim/terrain_object.rs) · [Smudges](../../src/sim/smudge_grid.rs) |
| What do a map trigger, the renderer and radar observe? | The existing bridge operation raises the applicable FA2 cell Tag at its defined position in the damage sequence. Presentation consumes published tile and object changes. A camera position, radar redraw or preview cannot cause a simulation event. [Bridge notifications](../../src/sim/world/bridge_ordinary_publication.rs) · [Radar updates](../../src/render/radar/terrain_updates.rs) |

**Bridge collapse and repair.** On a map with a vehicle on the deck and an object below, damage the bridge during a turn. Use the existing damage operation to apply span and occupant changes in order, including nested effects. During the span traversal, eligible cell Tags receive FA2 event 31 synchronously. A map action that queries a cell at that point sees the state reached so far. Check each occupant against its own damage and layer rules. Repair from the hut and check the ordered connections at the bridge ends before sending the vehicle across again. A save between collapse and repair must preserve pending fallout, occupancy order and navigation history that affects future movement. [Bridge damage](../../src/sim/world/bridge_orchestrator.rs) · [FA2 event dispatch](../../src/sim/trigger_runtime/dispatch.rs) · [Repair and navigation](../../src/sim/world/bridge_zone_publication.rs) · [State hash](../../src/sim/world/world_hash.rs)

**Ground deformation.** With the existing deformation option enabled, an admitted impact can lower ground and change neighboring ramps. Today's crater republishes changed cells, lighting and radar. Its clear and ramp tiles remain passable. A preset that changes passability must also invalidate the affected connectivity before the next dependent query, including a split on the far side of a bottleneck. Check the next move and foundation placement against the new surface. Verify the same crater after save/load and on a second peer. With deformation disabled, compare the original surface and the simulation RNG stream. ## Phase one in VERA Mod Engine

| Step | Change and retirement condition |
|---|---|
| Choose content | Ship independently authored RA2 and YR root presets. An Ares preset pins a YR version and adds that Ares version's defaults, tags and map instructions. The installation supplies read-only media, tiles and selected maps. A mod's declared INIs and the selected map's embedded rules retain the chosen dialect's replacement and overlay order. Remove implicit stock INI lookup from each migrated entrance, including the browser's file import and the room check. |
| Freeze the scenario | The package-qualified name identifies a definition for an author. The existing typed handle identifies it within one match. Keep a version-specific mapping for legacy roster and map ordinals, and a separate stable ID for each live instance. Resolve named declarations and forward references before spawning. Move each migrated field from native per-pass processing into the existing `RuleSet` projection once, removing its second read from merged INI. Bind the result to `SimResources` for preview, play, save and replay with the appropriate source chronology. [Rule processing](../../src/rules/process_owner.rs) · [Rule projection](../../src/rules/ruleset/loading.rs) · [Match resources](../../src/sim/runtime.rs) |
| Create and move a ground actor | A proposed `[Actor survey.Rover]` names its motion and occupancy. Its motion supplies terrain costs, speed, turning and arrival behavior. New declarations need no `[VehicleTypes]` entry or locomotor GUID. The legacy reader translates the selected stock ground controller into those inputs. Map placements and runtime-created actors use the same capability installation. An actor with movement can receive `Move` after the normal ownership and mission checks. A `Vehicle` filter flag on a stationary actor grants no movement. Remove the old category or GUID checks once every caller of the migrated operation uses the compiled motion. [Construction](../../src/sim/world/world_spawn/construction.rs) · [Move commands](../../src/sim/world/world_commands.rs) |
| Identify the match | Freeze preset versions, effective simulation inputs and the legacy ordering needed to resolve references. Reuse the rules digest and retain the separate map identity, build agreement and live state hash. The fork starts a new format for saves, replays and multiplayer. Reject an unsupported version before replacing a live match. A cold load restores the saved definitions, movement progress and authoritative map state, then rebuilds the derived indexes. [Saved scenario resources](../../src/sim/snapshot/scenario_resources.rs) · [Replay admission](../../src/sim/replay.rs) |

The first moving actor is an acceptance case for the architecture. In a second copy of its preset, change only the cost or permission for one terrain class. Keep the map, model, commands and seed fixed. Verify the changed route or arrival frame for both a map-placed actor and a newly created actor. Compare the stock profile against VERA20K for ground and bridge paths, cell occupancy, arrival frame and RNG draws.

## Acceptance and related work

- **Source isolation.** Run the fork with identical assets in two installations. Hide stock base INIs in one and change them in the other. Count requests for loose files and MIX members from first start, preview, start, cold load and room setup. Expect zero stock base INI requests and zero writes to either installation. The effective rules and results agree. An explicit mod or map override must still change its effective field and report its source. Missing packs report the required ID and version.
- **Determinism and scale.** Compare layer, position, trigger deliveries and occupancy order in continuous play and after a cold load. In the fork, two peers starting from the same new-format save and orders must produce matching state hashes. For the upstream retail path, account for the navigation hierarchy reconstructed by its save loader when comparing to uninterrupted play. A differing simulation input must fail room or replay admission. On the same machine, compare release builds on the 20,000-unit, 30-house march and record median frame time, P95, peak memory and the final state. These are proposed checks. [Save restore](../../src/sim/snapshot/simulation_restore.rs) · [Scale benchmark](../../src/bin/sim-bench.rs)
- **Mod authoring.** The fork starts with INI and INIWeaver. Scripted terrain actions could later use the same admission and mutation operations. Upstream RFC #4653 concerns a Wasm extension interface. HaoJun0823's content-package proposal and libi's reply on legacy INIs, gradual adoption and mod redistribution remain part of that discussion. The phase described here can be evaluated with one selected mod and one map.
